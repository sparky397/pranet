Pink ivory

Berchemia zeyheri (Sond.) Grubov · Rhamnaceae

Pink ivory, also called red ivory, purple ivory, unnini or umgoloti, is an African hardwood used to make a variety of products. The pink ivory tree grows predominantly in Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Northern Botswana and South Africa. The tree is protected and sustainably maintained in South Africa, only felled by very limited permit. The wood is extremely hard, with a density of 990 g/dm3.
