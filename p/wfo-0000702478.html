Gevuina avellana Molina · Proteaceae

Gevuina avellana, commonly known as the Chilean hazelnut or Gevuina hazelnut, is an evergreen tree growing up to 20 meters tall. It is the only species currently classified in the genus Gevuina belonging to the family Proteaceae. It is native to southern Chile and adjacent valleys in Argentina; it is found from sea level to 700 meters above sea level with a distribution that extends from 35° to 44° south latitude. Its indigenous name is guevin in Mapudungun.
