Cnidoscolus quercifolius Pohl · Euphorbiaceae

Cnidoscolus quercifolius is a species of flowering plant. It is endemic to Brazil. Its distributional range includes Bahia, Pernambuco, and Piauí. Its common name is the source of the term "favela" for a type of low-income informal settlement in Brazil.
