Baeckea frutescens L. · Myrtaceae

Baeckea frutescens is a species of flowering plant in the family Myrtaceae and is native to eastern southeastern China, Indochina, Malesia, New Guinea, and eastern Australia. It is a shrub with arching branches, linear leaves and white flowers with seven to thirteen stamens.
