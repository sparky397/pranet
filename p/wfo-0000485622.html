Broad-leaved Yellowwood

Podocarpus latifolius (Thunb.) R.Br. ex Mirb. · Podocarpaceae

Podocarpus latifolius is a large coniferous tree growing up to 35 m tall and 3 m in trunk diameter. It is the type species of the genus Podocarpus.
