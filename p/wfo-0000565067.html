Bhesa robusta (Roxb.) Ding Hou · Centroplacaceae

Bhesa robusta is a species of plant in the Centroplacaceae family. It is a tree native to Indochina, southeastern China, Nepal and the eastern Himalayas, Bangladesh, Peninsular Malaysia, Sumatra, and Borneo. It is widespread in tropical and subtropical moist lowland forests, typically dipterocarp forests, up to 1,100 metres (3,610 ft) elevation.
