Gowok

Syzygium polycephalum (Miq.) Merr. & L.M.Perry · Myrtaceae

Syzygium polycephalum, the gowok, is a species of flowering plant in the family Myrtaceae. It is native to western and central Malesia; Sumatra, Borneo, Java, the Lesser Sunda Islands, and Sulawesi. A tree reaching 8 to 20 m, it is typically found in secondary forests at elevations from 200 to 1,800 m. Its fruit and its young violet-colored leaves are edible and gathered from the wild and from cultivated specimens by local peoples. In particular, the fruit is used to make a "highly esteemed" jelly. It has been assessed as Near Threatened due to deforestation.
