Lasiurus scindicus Henrard · Poaceae

Lasiurus is a genus of Asian and African plants in the grass family, Poaceae, found primarily in arid regions. The only known species is Lasiurus scindicus, native to drier regions of northern Africa and southwestern Asia, from Mali, Morocco, and the Levant, all the way to India.formerly included
