Sterculia africana (Lour.) Fiori · Malvaceae

Sterculia africana is a species of flowering plant in the family Malvaceae. This deciduous tree is sometimes referred to by the common names African star-chestnut and mopopaja tree. It is distributed throughout Northeast Africa to Arabia.
