Yellow oleander ☠

Cascabela thevetia (L.) Lippold · Apocynaceae

Cascabela thevetia is a species of poisonous plant in the dogbane family Apocynaceae, native throughout Mexico and Central America and cultivated widely as an ornamental. It is a relative of Nerium oleander and thus is known by the common name yellow oleander.
