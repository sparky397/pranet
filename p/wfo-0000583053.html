Campnosperma auriculatum Hook.f. · Anacardiaceae

Campnosperma auriculatum is a rainforest tree in the family Anacardiaceae native to the East Indies and Southeast Asia. The sapling tree has very large leaves up to 1.8 metres long by up to 37 centimetres wide with wavey margins. The leaves of mature trees are oblanceolate and about 75 centimetres long, forming a tight circle at the ends of the branches. The flowers are yellowish, in small panicles, forming reddish berries upon pollination.
