Acacia macdonnelliensis

Acacia macdonnelliensis Maconochie · Fabaceae

Acacia macdonnelliensis, commonly known as MacDonnell mulga or Hill mulga, is a species of flowering plant in the family Fabaceae and is endemic to central Australia. The Alyawarr people know the plant as irrar, the Kaytetye know it as arleth-arlethe or arwele arleth-arlethe and the Western Arrernte people know it as irrkwarteke. It is a shrub or tree with deeply fissured grey bark, erect, very narrowly elliptic to linear or thread-like phyllodes, dense spikes of golden yellow flowers, and linear, papery pods slightly constricted between the seeds.
