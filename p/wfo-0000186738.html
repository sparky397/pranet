Guapuruvu tree

Schizolobium parahyba (Vell.) S.F.Blake · Fabaceae

Schizolobium parahyba, the Brazilian firetree, or Brazilian fern tree, is a species of tree from tropical America. It is the sole species in genus Schizolobium. It is notable for its fast growth. Under optimal conditions it may reach 30 meters high in only five years, which would make it one of the fastest growing trees ever.
