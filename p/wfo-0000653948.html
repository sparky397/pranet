Dombeya rotundifolia (Hochst.) Planch. · Malvaceae

Dombeya rotundifolia, the dikbas or "South African wild pear", is a species of small deciduous tree with dark grey to blackish deeply fissured bark, found in Southern Africa and northwards to central and eastern tropical Africa. Formerly placed in the Sterculiaceae, that artificial group has now been abandoned by most authors and the plants are part of an enlarged Malvaceae.
